The Dose Problem: Why Ingredient Count Matters Less Than You Think

Here's the critical issue that most reviews overlook: with 57 ingredients dividing a 3.5-gram blend, simple math suggests most ingredients are receiving only 60-80 milligrams per serving — well below clinically relevant amounts.

Vitamin C studies show that 75-90mg daily supports basic immune function, but amounts above 200mg show limited additional benefit. Zinc studies showing immune support typically use 15-25mg daily. Elderberry trials use concentrated extracts, not dried berry powder included in multi-ingredient blends.

This doesn't mean Tonic Greens is ineffective. It means the formula trades depth for breadth — spreading ingredients across multiple benefit categories rather than delivering clinically meaningful doses of any single compound.

Important Note About Proprietary Blends

Proprietary blends are legal, but they limit independent verification of efficacy. Without knowing exact doses, consumers cannot compare the formula to clinical research or make informed decisions about whether the product is likely to work for their specific needs.
